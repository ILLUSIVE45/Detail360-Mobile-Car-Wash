About Detail360 Mobile Car Wash

Our Story

Detail360 Mobile Car Wash was founded by Thabo, a student entrepreneur from Pretoria West. While studying, he noticed a problem that many people around him were facing every single week.

Residents living in flats and apartments do not have access to an outdoor water tap or a hose pipe, which makes washing a car at home almost impossible. Other residents own homes but work long hours, and cannot afford to spend an afternoon waiting in a queue at a car wash.

Detail360 was started to solve exactly that problem. Instead of asking the customer to bring the car to the wash, we bring the wash to the car – complete with our own water, equipment and cleaning products – directly to the customer's parking bay or driveway.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To deliver high quality and affordable car cleaning services directly to our customers' homes, saving them time and eliminating the struggle of waiting in long queues for a car wash.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and reliable mobile car wash brand in Pretoria West, recognised for punctuality and superior customer service.

How We Deliver Our Service

Everything we need travels with us, so there is nothing for you to prepare before we arrive:

  • We arrive with our own water supply, so no tap or hose pipe is needed.
  • We use waterless and low-water cleaning methods where possible, which is important in a water-scarce province.
  • We work quietly and neatly, so we are welcome in complexes and basement parking.
  • We arrive inside the time slot you booked, because punctuality is part of our vision.
  • We clean up completely after ourselves before we leave.

Who We Serve

  • Busy working professionals living in flats and apartments around Pretoria West.
  • Families with more than one vehicle in residential homes.
  • Anyone who would rather spend their Saturday morning at home than in a car wash queue.
