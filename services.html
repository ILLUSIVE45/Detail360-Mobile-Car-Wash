Good to Know

  • Prices include our own water, equipment and cleaning products.
  • We travel free of charge anywhere in Pretoria West. A small travel fee may apply outside our service area.
  • Payment is made after the job is finished, by cash or instant EFT.
  • A standard wash takes about 45 minutes. A valet or deep interior clean takes longer.
  • Booking more than one vehicle at the same address? Mention it in your enquiry and we will quote you.
